The Motivation and Mission

As an undergraduate researcher, you are often thrown into the world of research with little understanding of how it works. Fortunately, an incredible amount of knowledge is freely available online through academic journals and scientific publications. However, finding, reading, and understanding primary research articles can be challenging, especially for students who are new to research.

Equally important, and often even more difficult, is communicating what you learn to others. The ability to critically analyze scientific literature, identify key findings, and clearly explain complex concepts is essential for success in research. Whether you plan to continue in research, attend graduate school, pursue medicine, or enter industry, these skills will remain valuable throughout your career.

Badger Journals was created to help undergraduate students develop these abilities. Through reading, discussing, and writing about scientific research, members gain experience interpreting scientific literature while staying informed about the latest discoveries across a range of fields. Our goal is to make cutting-edge research more accessible while helping students become stronger scientists and communicators.
